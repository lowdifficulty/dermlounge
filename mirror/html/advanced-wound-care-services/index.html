Advanced Wound Care Services in Orange County, Los Angeles & Riverside County

DermLounge provides advanced wound care services for patients with chronic, non-healing, and complex wounds. Our team delivers care both in-clinic and through mobile bedside visits, ensuring patients receive consistent treatment without unnecessary hospitalizations.

Led by a Board-Certified Dermatologist and Mohs Surgeon, our wound care specialists treat a wide range of wounds using evidence-based protocols, advanced debridement techniques, and skin grafting when clinically appropriate. Services are often fully covered by Medicare and Medi-Cal for qualifying patients.

Types of Wounds We Treat

DermLounge specializes in high-risk and hard-to-heal wounds, including:

  • Pressure-Related Wounds (Pressure Ulcers and Bedsores)
  • Circulation-Related Ulcers (Venous and Arterial Ulcers)
  • Surgical and Post-Procedure Wounds
  • Traumatic Wounds (Injury-Related Wounds)
  • Moisture-Related Skin Breakdown (MASD and Incontinence-Associated Wounds)
  • Infected or Inflammatory Wounds
  • Chronic Non-Healing Wounds

Our clinicians focus on early intervention and consistent monitoring to prevent complications such as infection, hospitalization, or amputation.

Why Advanced Wound Care Matters

Chronic wounds affect millions of patients nationwide and place a significant burden on individuals and the healthcare system. Nearly 15% of Medicare beneficiaries suffer from chronic non-healing wounds, contributing to billions in annual healthcare costs.

With proper wound management:

  • Hospitalizations can be reduced by up to 88 percent
  • Amputation risk can be reduced by over 70 percent
  • Advanced skin grafts achieve closure in approximately 85 percent of eligible cases within three months

Consistent, specialized wound care leads to better healing outcomes and improved quality of life.

Our Wound Care Services

DermLounge provides comprehensive wound care services, including:

Bedside Wound Debridement

Removal of non-viable tissue to promote healing and reduce infection risk.

Ongoing Wound Monitoring and Dressing Changes

Patients are typically seen one to two times per week for reassessment, wound measurement, and treatment adjustments.

Advanced Skin Grafting

Eligible patients may receive skin grafting after four to six weeks of wound preparation, supporting faster and more complete healing.

Patient and Caregiver Education

We provide education on wound care management, skin protection, and prevention strategies to reduce recurrence.
