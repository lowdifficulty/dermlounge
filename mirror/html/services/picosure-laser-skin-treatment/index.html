What is Picosure Laser Skin Treatment?

Picosure Laser Skin Treatment is an advanced laser technology designed to address multiple skin concerns with minimal downtime. Using ultra-short pulses of energy, it stimulates collagen production and breaks down pigmentation, delivering noticeable results without damaging the surrounding tissue.
This non-invasive procedure is ideal for those seeking to rejuvenate their skin and improve its texture, tone, and overall radiance.

How Does Picosure Laser Skin Treatment Work?

The Picosure laser uses ultra-fast pulses of energy to shatter unwanted pigmentation and stimulate natural collagen production. This process targets problem areas deep within the skin while leaving the surrounding tissue unaffected, ensuring a safe and effective treatment.

Each session is customized to your skin type and concerns, delivering noticeable improvements after just one treatment, with optimal results seen after a series of sessions.

What to Expect During and After Treatment

During the Procedure:

  • The treatment is quick, typically lasting 20-30 minutes.
  • A cooling device ensures your comfort throughout the session.
  • The laser targets specific areas, making the procedure precise and effective.

After the Procedure:

  • Slight redness or sensitivity may occur but resolves within a few hours.
  • You can resume normal activities immediately, with no downtime required.
  • Results will gradually improve over several weeks as collagen production continues.

Preparation and Aftercare Tips

How to Prepare for Your Picosure Laser Treatment:

  • Avoid sun exposure and tanning beds for at least 2 weeks prior to treatment.
  • Discontinue use of retinol or exfoliating products 3-5 days before your session.
  • Ensure your skin is clean and free of makeup on the day of the appointment.

Aftercare Instructions:

  • Apply a gentle moisturizer or aloe vera to soothe the skin.
  • Use a broad-spectrum SPF 30+ sunscreen to protect treated areas.
  • Avoid exfoliation, hot showers, or strenuous exercise for 24-48 hours.
