Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Medicare cover wound care at DermLounge?
Yes. DermLounge wound care services are fully covered by Medicare for qualifying patients, with no out-of-pocket cost. We also accept Medi-Cal. Coverage eligibility is verified during the referral and intake process.
Do you offer mobile wound care for nursing home patients?
Yes. Our clinicians provide mobile bedside wound care at skilled nursing facilities, assisted living communities, and private residences throughout Orange, Los Angeles, and Riverside Counties.
How quickly can I get an appointment?
We prioritize wound care patients and typically offer same-week appointments. Call (714) 798-2900 or submit a consultation request above and our team will reach out within one business day.
What is debridement, and will I need it?
Debridement is the removal of dead or infected tissue from a wound to allow healthy tissue to grow. It is one of the most effective methods for promoting wound healing and is performed when clinically appropriate during your treatment.
How long does wound healing take?
Healing time depends on the wound type, size, and underlying health conditions. Most chronic wounds show measurable improvement within 4–6 weeks with consistent specialist care. Skin grafting may accelerate closure in eligible patients.
